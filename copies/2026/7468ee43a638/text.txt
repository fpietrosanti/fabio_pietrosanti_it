 MXMap Italia: Observatory of Digital Sovereignty of Italian Public Agencies

 Monitoring Italian Public Agencies Digital Sovereignty Status trough observation of Email System DNS Record

 Auditorium (A2) 

 14:20 35 mins 14/11/2026 

 MXMap Italia has been established as national observatory of the status of digital Sovereignty of italian public agencies, by analyzing DNS records on the basis of the MXMap software.

 The technical platform provides opendata, mapping and analytics of the 23k+ public agencies on https://mxmap.it

 The alarming results, with more than 46% of italian government agencies accessible through Cloud Act by USA Security Agencies, require all the institutional, technical, activist world to act now!

 The political platform https://osservatorio.mxmap.it aims to provides awareness across all the stakeholders, with policy and technical papers, briefs for decision makers, and periodic massive emailing campaign to inform the key persons at each agency (DPO, but also RTD, owner of the digital transformation).

 This talk wants to share the project results, methodology and a call to action to contribute to the project goals, improving the status of digital sovereignty with self-hosted infrastructure and free software.

 Speaker

 Fabio Pietrosanti (naif)

 Hermes Center for Transparency and Digital Human Rights
