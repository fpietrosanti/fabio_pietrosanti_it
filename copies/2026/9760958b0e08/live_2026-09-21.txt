 What it is

 Document your protocol. Measure it. Share it.

 Today a biohacker's protocol lives in spreadsheets, forum posts and chat prompts — not comparable, not followable, not readable by a physician. BIOHACK.IT turns it into structured, shareable data.

 01 — Document What you take & do
 Substances, therapies, exercise and nutrition — with dose, timing and real cycle schemas. Create from scratch, or copy any public protocol with one click.

 02 — Measure Efficacy & safety
 Biomarkers with reference, optimal and safety ranges — plus biological clocks (PhenoAge, epigenetic, glycation). Upload your past lab reports and get your history in graphs, free.

 03 — Share Open by default
 Publish your protocol and its outcomes. Follow and copy the people who get results, seeing their public biomarkers. Quality comes from three layers: deterministic validation, community review, expert oversight .

 What makes it computable

 The same protocol, written three ways, is one entity.

 This is the whole project in three lines. Today a dose lives as prose in a forum post, a screenshot, or a spreadsheet nobody else can read. We turn it into something a machine — and a researcher — can actually use.

 The plan

 From one person to a distributed trial.

 We are building in the open. The full functional specification — 26 capabilities, 150+ requirements across protocols, biomarkers, safety, community and open data — is public and browsable, and grows with community review. What we build first is deliberately much narrower than what we have specified.

 PHASE 0
 Foundations
 Domain model, biomarker registry, coded catalogue, safety rules.

 MVP
 Useful to one
 Protocol · Blood Layer · Safety · Doctor sheet — value in ten minutes, zero spend.

 NEXT
 Adherence
 Daily log — thirty seconds a day, and the reason any later number means something.

 THEN
 Useful to many
 Public protocols, follow & copy, safety on copy, pre-registered N-of-1.

 THEN
 Open data
 Cohorts, self-generated open & linked data, then a research API.

 DESTINATION
 Distributed trials
 Shared protocols with endpoints declared in advance, meeting a published bar.

 Deliberately deferred until those land: procurement, advanced nutrition, therapeutics scheduling, genomics beyond import, agent access. Breadth in thinking, discipline in shipping. 

 Rigor is a design decision, not a disclaimer. The platform describes, never prescribes; every claim is graded to its evidence. 

 The LLM writes words, never numbers. Extraction, thresholds and indices are deterministic and reproducible. 

 Safety is not opt-in. Follow a protocol and you inherit its safety markers and a mandatory baseline. 

 Open by code and by data. AGPL software; the public dataset is a clonable snapshot anyone can self-host.

 About

 The Software and the Foundation.

 biohack.it is an effort begun in 2022 by Fabio Pietrosanti — a hacker who turned to biology when his mother died of lung cancer. That loss set his focus on the biology of aging; he became a biohacker himself, and set out to give the international community the instrument he wished for himself in hacking his own biology.

 BIOHACK.IT
 The software and the initiative : an open platform for the biohacking and longevity community to document protocols and measure them, together, in the open.

 Hacking Biology Foundation
 The non-profit foundation behind the initiative — it manages the project and its development, guarantees it never becomes commercial, and pursues social objectives in the service of biohackers and longevity.

 We build on the shoulders of others and act as a connector of their data effort: Evipedia ( Forever Healthy , CC BY 4.0 ), the AI4L evidence framework, get-based , and open vocabularies ( LOINC , UCUM , RxNorm , OMOP , FHIR ). The community already exists on Rapamycin News ; now it gets its instrument.
