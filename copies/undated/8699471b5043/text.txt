 PrivateWave is an unfunded company based in Milan (Italy), founded in 2005 by Fabio Pietrosanti . It operates as a Provider of secure communication channel . PrivateWave has not raised any funding yet . The company has 1208 active competitors, including 164 funded and 147 that have exited . Its top competitor s include companies like Vonage , Mitel and LumApps . 
 Company Details
 Private Wave provides a secure communication channel for calls and messages. The company provides an on premise solution and software as a service. Private Wave as a service uses end to end encryption to provide secure messages and phone calls. PrivateWave on premise provides confidentiality to enterprise communications and protection against wiretapping.
