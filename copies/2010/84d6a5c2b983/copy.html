Turbo codes.ppt

Turbo codes are a type of error correcting code that can achieve performance close to the theoretical maximum allowed by Shannon's limit. Turbo codes use an iterative decoding process between two recursive systematic convolutional encoders separated by an interleaver. Spread spectrum modulation is a wideband modulation technique that provides three main advantages over fixed frequency transmission: resistance to noise/interference, difficulty intercepting signals, and allowing multiple transmissions to efficiently share frequencies.
