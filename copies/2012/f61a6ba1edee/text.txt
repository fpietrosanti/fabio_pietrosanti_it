 How The Great Firewall of China Is Blocking Tor

 The paper investigates the mechanisms used by the Great Firewall of China (GFC) to block access to the Tor network, revealing that the GFC employs dynamic blocking techniques rather than simple IP blacklisting. It discusses the results of experiments conducted to analyze the blocking infrastructure, including the identification of active scanning methods and the behavior of blocked bridges. The authors propose circumvention techniques and emphasize the importance of understanding the GFC's capabilities for future efforts to bypass censorship.
