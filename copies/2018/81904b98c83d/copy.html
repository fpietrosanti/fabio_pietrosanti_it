Our Mission

The World Bank estimates that the annual global economy for whistleblowing is over $ 1.5 trillion and less than 2% of it is actually revealed.

Financial crimes and corporate malpractices has a negative impact on health outcomes, infant mortality, high order crimes, tax evasion, human trafficking, etc. that weighs on everyone’s wealth.

Ethic Whispers aims to increase whistleblowing reports pursuing financial rewards.

Whistleblowing Rewards

Many countries in the world,  particularly in U.S. pays those who reports corporate malpractice, financial crime, worldwide, also on foreign companies outside their borders. Often whistleblowers remain in silence because of this 3 reasons


Identity disclosure
Reporting through Whistleblowing Reward Programs require to disclose whistleblower identity.

Time and money
Filling whistleblower reports, taking care of the legal proceeding of the investigations is costly and time expensive.

Lack of information
All informed citizens could potentially report through Whistleblowing Reward Programs. They just don’t know about it at all.

Ethic Wispers


Identity disclosure
Keeps the identity of the whistleblower secret and pay in crypto.

Time & Money
Fully manages all operations required from the first whistleblower’s submission up to the reward payment, including relocation services for him and his family.

Lack of information
Will run multiple communication campaigns in different countries to raise awarness and engage potential whistleblowers. Campaign targets are defined by foreign owned companies of US regulated stock market entities.

How it works

EthicWhispers engage not yet aware whistleblowers to collect corporate malpractices and financial crimes information, investigating the facts and then legally reporting to the authorities.

Whistleblowing reward programs pay between 10% to 30% of the money recovered by the government through fines and sanctions.

EthicWhispers manage legally, fiscally and financially the reward collection and redistribution among the stackholders.

All of that ensuring whistleblower’s anonymity, including relocation assistance in establishing a new life for him and his family.
