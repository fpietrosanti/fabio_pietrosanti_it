Prevent CRIME attack on TLS by disabling compression
https://github.com/tor2web/Tor2web/issues/120
opened 2013-09-10T07:39:06Z by fpietrosanti · state closed

From Tor2web mailing list, by Tom Ritter.

CRIME exploits compression, not a particular cipher.  Somewhere in [0]
you need to disable compression.  The relevant option for OpenSSL is
SSL_OP_NO_COMPRESSION.  In theory you'd add
ctx.set_options(SSL.SSL_OP_NO_COMPRESSION), but in practice, pyOpenSSL
may not define that option.

-tom

[0] https://github.com/globaleaks/Tor2web-3.0/blob/master/tor2web/utils/ssl.py#L60
