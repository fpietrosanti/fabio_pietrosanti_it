A meeting of the core Tor team, developers, volunteers and invited guests in or near Amsterdam, to discuss plans, milestones, deadlines, and other important matters.

Participant Guidelines: https://trac.torproject.org/projects/tor/wiki/org/meetings/2017Amsterdam/ParticipantGuidelines 

 Week at a glance

 Wed, 22 Mar : Team members arrive (teams decide who to invite)

 Thu, 23 Mar : Teams meet to work on mapping and other issues; general meeting invitees arrive; group dinner in the evening
 10.00 - 18.30: Self-organized sessions: https://trac.torproject.org/projects/tor/wiki/org/meetings/2017Amsterdam/23March 

 19:00: Group dinner in Arena’s Chapel (main meeting room). 

 Fri, 24 Mar : Plenary session for strategic planning; discussion on how we want Tor to grow and what resources we’ll need to get there; Tor board open meeting
 10.00 Opening session

 10.45 Strategic Planning sessions #1 : What does the future look like?

 12.30 Lunch

 13.30 Strategic Planning sessions #2 : What could Tor look like?

 15.00 Break

 15.30 Open Board Meeting

 16.30 Adjourn

 Sat, 25 Mar : Structured agenda with specific tracks; this part of the meeting is open to anyone in the public with advance notice (registration required)

 Sun, 26 Mar : Everyone heads home or hangs around for hacking

 Mon, 27 Mar : Everyone heads home or hangs around for more hacking
 IPv6 Hackfest 

Note that this schedule is a bit different than agendas of the past. We’re responding to feedback requesting more focused work and planning. While we enjoy working with the general public, there’s so much going on at Tor meetings, we’re limiting involvement to invited guests and those who have told us in advance of their plans to join us on the open day. Tor Project members wanting even more open public involvement are invited to host something on the 27th or 28th or to hold or attend hacking events separate from the Tor meeting throughout the year. Please note also that this is an experiment. If we feel like this agenda isn’t working, we can tweak it before the next meeting in the fall.

 Location

Hotel Arena: ​ https://www.hotelarena.nl/vergaderruimtes-amsterdam 

 Airport

The airport code for flight booking is 'AMS'. Watch-out for any taxi scams at the airport. Shouldn't be more than 50 euro to the city center. (But who would take a taxi from the airport, when there are such great trains for 5 euros?)
There are quite a few train/bus options that will get you to Hotel Arena. There is even a hotel shuttle bus that will take you directly to the hotel. Here is a link to the various transportation options. ​ https://www.hotelarena.nl/en/contact 

But there are also trains for many nearby countries.
