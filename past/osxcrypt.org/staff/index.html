Staff

Well,

the staff of osxcrypt is the opensource community. OSXCrypt started with a community driven funding, is opensource and will go on with a community driven project with the support (like for the initial funding) provide by the so many people around the world that perceive the social need of privacy protection.

Remember that privacy is one of the most important value in the modern information society.

However, who we are right now?

We are now 3 people (in strictly alphabetical order):

Orlando Bassotto (nextie) - The Hacker and the Developer .

Matteo Flora (lk) - Starter of the project .

Fabio Pietrosanti (naif) - Starter of the project .

The Funders - Who donated their money to have this project became reality. Thank you funders!

We want the staff to grow, please support the OSXCrypt Project!

If you want to propose some feature, cooperate with the development, bugfix the code, enhance it subscribe to the Development Mailing List of OSXCrypt Project!

If you want support the development of some feature by donating some money or financing some feature please write us to info A-T osxcrypt.org .


  • About

    OsxCrypt.org is an open effort for porting OpenSource encryption tools on MacOsX with seamingless integration with the OS. It is run by the Staff and thanks to the donations of the Funders.
