About

OSXCrypt will become the leading opensource encryption platform for Mac OS X.

It will support multiple encryption systems with first goal to support TrueCrypt volumes.We expect to get community support to provide Mac users access to dmcrypt, luks, loop-aes and pgp encrypted disks.

Regarding truecrypt, the first supported platform…TrueCrypt is the leading product for encryption and plausible deniability. it comes in Windows and Linux flavors and is perfectly multi platform and usable.
The actual implementation, though, isn’t compatible with MacOs X and multiple contacts with developers have resulted in nothing at all.
