Information Security, an unconventional approach

Fabio Pietrosanti, Senior Security Analyst, is involved professionally in Information Security since 1998.

In more than six years of activity, Fabio has been working for public and private organizations assuming different roles in different areas of the IT and information security.

Since the age of 18 he has been involved in spreading the Information Security culture, publishing technical articles on specialized magazines working with radios, televisions and national newspapers, attending on topics like privacy, IT security, hacking and cybercrime.

He contributed to events and organizations like Centro Alti Studi Lotta al Terrorismo, Cattaneo Castellanza University, IBM, Clusit, Infosecurity, Webbit.

Penetration Testing, Investigative Support, Specialized Training, IT Infrastructures Analysis and Planning, Applied Privacy are the main topics on which Fabio proposes himself as indipendant partner.


"The unconventional approach to the technology, the ability to quickly analyze and correlate the multitude of elements characterizing an organization and an information system are key prerequisites of the Information Security Analyst.

When the strategist and hacker's mind get together . . ."

Fabio Pietrosanti
